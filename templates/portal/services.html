NACB services are available online, 24/7. Prefer help in person? Visit NACB headquarters or your district office and an officer will capture your details for you.

National Artist Register

Individuals and organisations in all art forms can register, maintain a digital portfolio and choose whether to appear in the public Artist Directory.

  • Online registration and verification
  • Unique NACB registration number
  • Portfolio of images, video, audio and documents
  • Privacy controls over what is public
Register now

Grants & funding

Apply for the Arts and Culture Grant and future instruments such as the Botswana Creative Arts Fund through one online form.

  • Multi-step form with draft saving
  • Automatic eligibility & document checks
  • Status tracking and SMS / email updates
View grant calls

Events, competitions & festivals

Register for workshops, training, ward and district competitions of the National Arts Festival, exhibitions and more.

  • Calendar filtered by type, district and date
  • Automatic waitlist when events are full
  • Add events to your phone calendar
Browse events

Endorsement & support letters

Registered creatives can request NACB letters of support for visas, festivals, funding applications, permits and residencies.

  • Target turnaround: 10 working days
  • Track progress online
  • Letters carry a verification code
Request a letter
