Endorsement & support letters

Registered creatives can request an official NACB letter of support for travel, festivals, funding applications, permits, competitions and residencies.

Who can apply

You must be a verified member of the National Artist Register. If you are not yet registered, create an account and complete your profile first.

What you'll need

  • Name and address of the organisation the letter should be addressed to (e.g. an embassy or festival)
  • The event, programme or opportunity and its dates
  • A short justification explaining how the opportunity benefits your practice and Botswana's creative sector
  • A supporting document such as an invitation or acceptance letter (recommended)

Turnaround

NACB aims to respond within 10 working days. Submit early if you need the letter for a visa application.

Authenticity

Every letter carries a reference and a verification code. Anyone can confirm a letter is genuine on the verification page.
